## Interpretation

- Step-savings usually reduces planner calls and measured library staleness after repeated changes.
- Execution steps, aborts and success are not uniformly better across cycles or world sizes. The 24-state system still shows late-cycle route-quality risk.
- The savings score should therefore remain opt-in until freshness / regime-change handling is strengthened and revalidated.
- No formal significance tests were run; these are descriptive paired-seed results.
